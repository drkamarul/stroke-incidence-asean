## Headline

In 2026, the **crude national stroke incidence** in Malaysia was **162.5 per 100,000** (Wilson 95% CI 161.1-163.8), based on 56,515 incident stroke cases over a population of 34,785,153.

## 2016-2026 national trend

Between 2016 and 2026, Malaysia's crude stroke incidence changed from 152.0 to 162.5 per 100,000, a 6.9% increase over the eleven-year window. See `malaysia_national_trend.png` for the year-by-year trajectory with Wilson 95% CI ribbon.

## Methods note

- Crude annual incidence per 100,000 computed as (new stroke cases / mid-year population) x 100,000.
- 95% confidence intervals: **Wilson Score** interval (better small-sample coverage than the Wald interval).
- Pipeline: R + tidyverse (dplyr/tidyr/readr/purrr) with ggplot2 + scales.
- R version: R version 4.1.2 (2021-11-01).
